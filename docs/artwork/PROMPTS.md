# Paper hands — image prompts

Built-in ImageGen was used (not CLI/API fallback). Original composition, two extraction attempts, and two chroma-background edits. The first extraction outputs painted checkerboards (RGB, no alpha), so the final edits use flat green; deterministic chroma extraction creates the actual RGBA cutouts without repainting the hands.

## Mother composition

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original layered hero illustration for a bright minimal personal website, TWO separate paper-cut illustrated hands in ONE unified composition on genuinely transparent background. Landscape 1536x1024 or wider. A refined editorial manga / graphic-novel ink illustration printed on slightly crumpled ivory-white paper, fine charcoal outlines, subtle crosshatching and halftone, tactile paper grain, visible delicate folds and creases catching soft studio light, restrained very pale blue/lilac edge shading. NOT flat emoji, not rubber gloves, not a 3D low-poly model, not photoreal skin.
Composition: reinterpret the reaching gesture of The Creation of Adam with ONLY two anatomically believable hands and generous forearm extensions, no people or religious scenery. First forearm enters from UPPER LEFT and hand extends DOWN AND RIGHT, with relaxed curved index finger pointing toward the center. Second forearm enters from LOWER RIGHT and hand extends UP AND LEFT, index finger reaching toward the other fingertip. A clear DIAGONAL upper-left to lower-right arrangement, NOT horizontal, NOT symmetrical mirrored copies. One gesture active and extended, the other gently relaxed. Both index fingertips near image center but separated by a clean gap of approximately 12% image width. Keep the hands in separate nonoverlapping halves so they can be extracted as independent layers. Each arm ends with an intentional rough torn-paper edge fully inside frame, not cut off by canvas; keep 4% transparent padding around each silhouette. Realistic five-finger anatomy, natural joints; other fingers softly curled and individually legible. No extra digits. Beautiful sculptural folds of crumpled paper running naturally through the illustrated hands; paper texture belongs to hand silhouettes ONLY. Moderate ink definition, enough contrast on a near-white website. Transparent outside the two paper silhouettes including between fingers. No rectangular paper sheet, no scene background, no checkerboard painted in, no typography, no stars, no glow, no particles, no watermark. Lighting and palette unified across the two hands.
